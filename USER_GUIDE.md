# Employee Time Clock - User Guide

Welcome to the new Time Clock application! This guide will walk you through how to use the app to track your time, submit procedures, and manage your shifts.

## 1. Logging In
- Open the application link provided to you.
- Enter your **Email Address** and your assigned **PIN**.
- Tap **Login** to access your dashboard.

## 2. Clocking In
- At the start of your shift, verify your current location is accurate, then tap the big blue **Clock In** button.
- *Note: Depending on your role, the application will record your timestamp securely to ensure accurate payroll.*

## 3. Tracking Procedures (If Applicable)
- While you are clocked in, you will see a list of available **Procedures**.
- As you complete tasks (e.g., specific exams or actions you are paid per-item for), tap the **Plus (+)** button next to the relevant procedure to record it.
- If you make a mistake, tap the **Minus (-)** button to remove a procedure count. 

## 4. Editing Your Clock-In Time
- If you forgot to clock in precisely when you started working, don't worry! 
- While clocked in, tap the **Edit Time** button next to your current clock-in time.
- Adjust the time to when you *actually* began working, provide a brief reason for the change, and save.

## 5. Clocking Out
- At the end of your shift, tap the red **Clock Out** button.
- **Forgot to Clock Out?** If you remain clocked in for more than 10 hours, the app will display a visual alert and send a browser notification (if you have notifications enabled) to remind you to clock out!
- If you forgot to clock out earlier, you can **edit the Clock-Out Time** directly in the confirmation popup before finalizing.
- Review your total hours and make any last-minute adjustments to the procedures you performed.
- Tap **Confirm Clock Out** to submit your shift. Your time and procedures are automatically synced with payroll.

## 6. Managing Past Shifts (Work History)
Your dashboard includes a **Work History** list showing your past shifts.
- **Add a Past Shift:** If you completely forgot to clock in and out for a shift, tap **Add Past Shift**. You can select your start and end times, enter the procedures you completed, and provide a reason.
- **Edit a Shift:** In the Work History list, tap the **Edit** (pencil) icon next to a completed shift if you need to fix a mistake in the times or procedure counts.
- **Delete a Shift:** If a shift was submitted in error (for example, a duplicate), tap the **Delete** (trash can) icon to remove it from your records.

## 7. Phone Day Bonus
- If you are eligible, you might see a **Record Phone Day** button on your dashboard.
- Tapping this securely logs your $50 Phone Day bonus for the pay period.

---
**Troubleshooting:**
- **App Offline?** The app is designed to sync when possible. If you temporarily lose connection, it will save your shift locally and automatically try to sync it once your connection is restored.
- **Need Help?** If you run into any persistent issues with logging in or submitting shifts, please reach out to management.
